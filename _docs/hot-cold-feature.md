## Overview

The Hot/Cold feature in Puree allows you to set delivery temperature options for your menu items. You can specify whether items should be delivered heated, chilled, or offer both options to your customers. This feature helps ensure food safety and customer satisfaction by clearly communicating temperature requirements throughout the ordering and delivery process.

## Setting Up Delivery Temperature Options

### Step 1: Access Menu Items

1. Navigate to your **Menu Items** section from the main dashboard
2. Select the item you want to configure for hot/cold delivery options
3. Click **Edit** on the selected item

### Step 2: Configure Delivery Temperature Options

1. In the item editing screen, locate the **Delivery Temperature Options** section
2. By default, this feature is set to **Off** - you must manually enable it for each item
3. Choose from the following options:
   - **Heated only** - Item will only be delivered hot
   - **Chilled only** - Item will only be delivered cold
   - **Both Heated and Chilled** - Customer can choose (most common option)

### Step 3: Save Your Changes

Click **Save** to apply the delivery temperature settings to your menu item.

## Using Hot/Cold Options in Quotes

### Adding Items with Temperature Options

1. When creating or editing a quote, add your configured menu items as usual
2. For items with delivery temperature options enabled, you'll see a **Delivery Temperature** field
3. This field is **compulsory** - you must select either "Delivered Heated" or "Delivered Chilled"
4. Select the appropriate temperature option based on customer requirements

### Temperature Display

Once saved, the temperature option will be displayed:
- On quotes: "Delivered Heated" or "Delivered Chilled" appears after the item name
- On dockets: Kitchen staff can see the temperature requirement
- On reports: Temperature information is included in all relevant reports
- On run sheets: Delivery staff can see temperature requirements for proper handling

## Best Practices

- **Enable for appropriate items**: Consider which items genuinely benefit from temperature control options
- **Use "Both" option**: Most items work well with both heated and chilled options, giving customers flexibility
- **Clear communication**: The temperature information helps ensure proper food handling throughout the delivery process
- **Kitchen coordination**: Ensure kitchen staff understand the temperature requirements shown on dockets
